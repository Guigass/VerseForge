# Vocabulario de estilo

Descritores em ingles para montar o campo Estilo. Escolher de 2 a 4 instrumentos e 1 ou 2 texturas; nao copiar a linha inteira.

## Voz

- **Genero e timbre:** male baritone, male tenor, raspy male, female alto, female soprano, airy female, soulful female, youthful, mature.
- **Entrega:** intimate, conversational, belted, laid-back, urgent, breathy, soulful, gritty, falsetto, spoken word, melodic rap, fast rap, chant.
- **Proximidade:** close-mic, dry, upfront, doubled, stacked harmonies, call-and-response, group chorus.
- **Idioma:** Brazilian Portuguese vocals; European Portuguese vocals; bilingual Portuguese and English.

## Groove e energia

- laid-back, swung, straight, syncopated, half-time, driving, mid-tempo, slow burn, head-nod, danceable, rubato.
- Arco: sparse verses, lifted chorus, stripped bridge, big final chorus, slow build, gradual fade.

## Producao e mix

- warm analog, tape saturation, vinyl warmth, crisp modern mix, lo-fi, live room sound, polished studio, wide stereo, bass-forward, vocal-forward, dry mix, lush reverb, spring reverb, tape delay.

## Epoca (em vez de nome de artista)

- late-1960s tropicalia, 1970s MPB studio sound, 1980s Brazilian pop rock, 1990s boom-bap, early-2000s R&B, 2010s indie.
