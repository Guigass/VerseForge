Brazilian boom bap hip-hop with a strong São Paulo identity, blending classic 90s rap production with subtle samba and MPB DNA. Heavy, deep sub-bass and punchy kick, dry snare, dusty MPC-style drums, vinyl crackle, chopped acoustic guitar or cavaquinho samples, warm Rhodes/piano, discreet brass stabs and occasional upright bass textures. 84–88 BPM. Male Brazilian Portuguese vocal, conversational storytelling flow, relaxed but confident, with internal rhymes and natural swing. Verses should feel intimate and cinematic, like walking through São Paulo at night. Chorus more melodic and instantly memorable, preserving the nostalgic feeling of a classic samba. Train station ambience and distant railway sounds in intro/outro. Raw, warm, organic production, no trap hi-hats, no EDM, no glossy pop. Huge low end, vintage sample character, soulful, nostalgic and urban.
