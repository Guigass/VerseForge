Dark soulful jazz rap with subtle reggae and dub influence. Deep warm sub-bass, punchy boom-bap drums, upright bass, offbeat reggae guitar, smoky piano, Rhodes, baritone sax and muted trumpet. Male Portuguese rap vocals, deep relaxed voice, reflective flow, melodic phrasing and natural internal rhymes. Around 86–90 BPM, minor key. Blend live jazz swing, hip-hop groove and laid-back reggae skank without becoming traditional reggae. Heavy dub-style bass, spacious echoes, dramatic pauses and sax call-and-response. Verses intimate and cinematic; chorus warmer, wider and emotional with layered vocals. Reggae-jazz breakdown with guitar, deep bass, sparse piano and sax before the full drums return. Analog warmth, nighttime-to-sunrise atmosphere, sophisticated, soulful and hopeful. No pop, no bright tropical sound, no trap hi-hat overload.
