Brazilian roots dub reggae with a modern, warm and hypnotic sound. Deep massive sub-bass as the main character, slow 74–78 BPM groove, heavy round bassline, classic offbeat reggae guitar, organic drums, rimshot snare, percussion, subtle organ bubbles and occasional melodica. Spacious dub production with long tape delays, spring reverb, echo throws, dropouts, filter sweeps and moments where instruments disappear leaving only bass and drums. Male Brazilian Portuguese vocal, relaxed and charismatic, alternating melodic reggae singing, conversational phrasing and short rhythmic rap passages. Reflective but uplifting mood: nighttime city, home, love, slowing down, curiosity, freedom and enjoying simple moments. Catchy singable chorus without becoming pop. Mid-song bass-heavy dub breakdown and a huge final chorus followed by an extended echo-drenched outro. Raw, organic, warm, psychedelic, intimate and extremely bass-forward.
