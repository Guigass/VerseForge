## ESTILO PARA O SUNO

Late-1970s British guitar rock, 148 BPM, tight straight 4/4 with subtle jazz swing, urban nighttime club atmosphere. Two electric guitars only: glassy single-coil lead with intricate plucked arpeggios, fast melodic fills and long virtuosic solos; dry syncopated rhythm guitar. Melodic moving bass, crisp closed hi-hat, punchy dry snare and restrained kick. Relaxed but precise human groove, warm analog recording, wide dynamic range. Mature male narrative vocal in Brazilian Portuguese, cool and conversational in the verses, stronger on “Donos do Balanço”. Explosive extended guitar solo and instrumental outro. No country, folk, Americana, acoustic strumming, slide guitar, blues shuffle, southern-rock twang, brass, synths, metal or pop production.
