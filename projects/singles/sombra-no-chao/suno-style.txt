Brazilian boom-bap hip-hop with MPB, neo-soul R&B and Brazilian rock, 90 BPM, dark head-nod swung groove. Dusty punchy drums, deep melodic electric bass, warm Rhodes with sophisticated jazz harmony, and expressive overdriven electric guitar answering the vocal. Deep male baritone vocals in Brazilian Portuguese, intimate conversational rap in the verses and soulful melodic singing in the chorus. Tense, nocturnal and defiant. Sparse close-mic verses, rising pre-chorus, wide layered chorus, stripped bridge and a gritty guitar climax. Warm analog, bass-forward, vocal-forward mix.
