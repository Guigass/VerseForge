Samba-rock with classic boom-bap hip-hop and MPB harmony, 90 BPM, warm syncopated swung groove. Dusty dry drums, round electric bass, nylon-string guitar, Rhodes and muted trumpet. Recurring duet in Brazilian Portuguese: warm female alto carries the ancestral lament in melodic verses; deep male baritone delivers grounded conscious rap; choruses join in close harmony. Solemn, proud and tender. Intimate verses, lifted choruses, stripped call-and-response bridge, fuller final chorus. Warm analog studio sound, bass-forward clear mix.
