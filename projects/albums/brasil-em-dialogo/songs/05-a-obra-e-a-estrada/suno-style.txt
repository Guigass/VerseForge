Samba-rock with MPB narrative harmony and restrained classic boom-bap, 84 BPM, dark swung groove. Dusty dry drums, round electric bass, nylon-string guitar, Rhodes, low muted trumpet and tenor sax tension. Recurring duet in Brazilian Portuguese: warm female alto leads the melodic story of a life assembled and lost; deep male baritone answers with grounded conscious rap from the road; choruses join in close harmony. Solemn, heavy and compassionate. Intimate verses, wider choruses, stripped bridge, fuller last chorus. Warm analog studio, bass-forward clear mix.
